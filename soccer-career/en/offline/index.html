Offline football career game

Careers in Football Career Sim are calculated on your phone, not a server. You can advance seasons, choose growth cards, transfer and retire without a connection, including on the subway or a plane. Registration and login are unnecessary.

As of November 2, 2026. Written by YT Lab, the makers of Football Career Sim: Be a Pro, using the game rules and careers actually completed by players.

What works offline

  • Create a career: name, nationality, position and stat dice
  • Advance seasons, monthly results and growth
  • Choose growth cards and use one free reroll per card
  • Request transfers, receive offers and renew contracts
  • Retire and receive a six-axis career evaluation

What needs a connection

  • Refresh rankings and Hall of Fame
  • Today's League and Daily Challenge
  • Start a career using start cards
  • Restore a cloud save
  • Store purchases, ad rewards, notices and inbox

Offline careers can reach the rankings

If you participate in rankings, a career retired offline is stored on the device and submitted when you reopen the app with a connection. Careers with no completed season are not submitted.

The weekly ranking restarts Monday 00:00 UTC. Players have submitted 36,402 careers to the rankings so far.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to log in?

No. Start immediately without registration or Apple or Google login.

Do offline careers appear in the rankings?

Yes. If you participate in rankings and have completed at least one season, the career is submitted the next time you open the app online.
