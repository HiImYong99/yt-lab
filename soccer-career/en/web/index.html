Play Football Career Sim in your browser

Try it in your browser now, without installing anything. The web demo is the same game as the mobile app: create a 17-year-old youth player and play through the first five seasons. No registration or login is required.

Play in your browserWorks in both desktop and mobile browsers. The game files may take a few seconds to download the first time.

As of November 2, 2026. Written by YT Lab, the makers of Football Career Sim: Be a Pro, using the game rules and careers actually completed by players.

What you can do in the web demo

  • Create a player by choosing a name, nationality and position (striker or goalkeeper), then rolling the stat dice
  • Play five seasons, following monthly match results and growth
  • Choose one of three growth cards at the start and every two seasons
  • Make your first-team debut, earn playing time and enter the summer transfer market

Return in the same browser to continue your player’s career.

Only in the app

  • The full career from season six through retirement, including your retirement evaluation
  • Rankings, Hall of Fame, Today's League and Daily Challenge
  • Defender, midfielder and winger positions, plus the store

Progress in the web demo does not transfer to the app. You start over with a new player in the app.

The demo is the same game

The web demo runs on the same code as the mobile app, not a separate mini-game. Growth rules, growth cards, playing time and transfer criteria are all the same, so what you learn in the demo also applies in the app.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. Both the web demo and the app are free. The app offers optional purchases.

Can I transfer my player from the demo to the app?

No. Players created on the web cannot be transferred to the app. You start with a new player in the app.

What devices can I play on?

You can play on a computer or phone with a modern browser. On a phone, the app is faster and lets you continue all the way to retirement.
