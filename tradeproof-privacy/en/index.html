YT Lab ("we") processes personal data of TradeProof app ("Service") users as follows.

1. Data we collect

DataPurposeRetention
Email addressAccount creation, sign-in, email verificationDeleted on account deletion
NicknameLeaderboard and profile displayDeleted on account deletion
Exchange API key (read-only)Balance/deposit-withdrawal lookup and return computationDeleted immediately on unlink or account deletion
Balance snapshots & holdingsMetrics (return, MDD) and ranking computationDeleted on account deletion

2. API key handling

  • Only read-only exchange API keys can be registered; keys with withdrawal or trading permission are rejected.
  • Keys are stored server-side encrypted with AES-256-GCM and are never stored on your device.
  • Keys are used solely to read balances and deposit/withdrawal history.

3. What is public

  • Only your nickname, percentage metrics (return, MDD) and a normalized index curve may be visible to other users.
  • Absolute amounts — balances and holdings — are never exposed.
  • Profile visibility can be changed in the app settings.

4. Processors and third parties

The Service uses Google Firebase (authentication, database, serverless functions; Google LLC) to process data. We do not sell or share personal data with any other third party.

5. Deletion

Using "Delete account" in the app permanently deletes your email, nickname, API key, snapshots and metric history immediately.

6. Your rights

You can unlink your exchange or delete your account at any time in the app, and contact us below for any privacy inquiry.

7. Disclaimer

All information in the Service is informational only and is not investment advice. You are solely responsible for your investment decisions.
