YT Lab ("we") operates the mobile game Deadline Day: Football Agent (the "app"). The app has no accounts and does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. Everything that leaves your device is listed below.

There is no free-text input. Agency names shown in the ranking are generated by the app from word lists.

2. Retention

Game progress is stored on your device (shared_preferences) and deleted with the app. Ranking records are kept until you ask us to delete them. Google Analytics event data is deleted automatically after at most 14 months.

3. Advertising

Ads are a bottom banner, one interstitial when closing a season summary every few seasons, and rewarded ads you choose to watch (re-drawing scouting candidates or cards). No ads appear during a negotiation. Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads; see Google's Privacy Policy. On iOS, personalised or non-personalised ads depend on your App Tracking Transparency choice; in Europe a consent form is shown first. Buying "Remove Ads" removes the banner and interstitials.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store / Google Play; we do not collect card or other financial information. Products: Remove Ads, scouting region packs and agency style packs (one-time, permanent) and gold (consumable). Odds for paid re-draws are shown in the app. Use "Restore Purchases" in Settings to restore purchases.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your information and share it with no one beyond the processing above (AdMob, app stores, our Cloudflare ranking server, Google Analytics for Firebase and Crashlytics), except where required by law.

6. Your choices

Deleting the app deletes the data on your device. Ranking participation and analytics can be turned off in Settings. To delete ranking records, email us with the anonymous id shown on the version screen in Settings.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
