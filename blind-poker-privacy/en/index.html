Blind Poker

Privacy Policy

Effective: July 21, 2026

한국어 · English

YT Lab ("we", "the developer") operates the mobile game Blind Poker ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app processes and how.

1. Information we process

The app has no sign-up (no email or phone number). It processes only the following:

ItemWhenPurpose
Anonymous account ID (auto-generated by Firebase Anonymous Auth)First use of online featuresKeep your online progress on the same device
Nickname & avatar (set by you)When you edit your profileShown to opponents and on leaderboards
Game progress (win/loss record, league points (RP), level/XP, stars & cosmetics, missions/achievements)During online playWeekly league ranking, saving progress
Match data (match state, betting actions)During online/friend matchesReal-time play (server-refereed)
Advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID) and ad-related dataWhen ads are shown (processed by Google AdMob)Ad serving & measurement
We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, contacts, or precise location. Practice matches against the AI run entirely on your device. The anonymous account is a random identifier that does not reveal who you are.

2. Storage

  • Online profile and match data are stored and processed on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & Cloud Functions, region: Seoul, asia-northeast3).
  • On-device data (settings, mute, mission cache, etc.) stays on your device.
  • Weekly league points reset every week; account progress is kept while the service operates.

3. Ads & purchases

  • The app shows Google AdMob ads (banner, interstitial, rewarded). AdMob may process advertising identifiers to serve ads. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • On iOS, personalized vs. non-personalized ads follow your App Tracking Transparency (ATT) choice.
  • "Remove Ads" is handled by Apple App Store / Google Play in-app purchase; we never see your payment details.
  • In-game chips and stars are score tokens only — they cannot be bought with or exchanged for real money.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the processors above (Firebase, AdMob, the app stores) as needed to run the app, or when required by law.

5. Your rights

  • You can change your nickname and avatar in the app at any time.
  • To delete your account (the anonymous ID and linked progress), email us below — we will delete it promptly.
  • Deleting the app removes all on-device data.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.
