Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026 (0.1)

한국어 · English

YT Lab ("we") operates the mobile game Hero Rogue (the "app"). The app has no accounts. What leaves the device is advertising data (Google AdMob — subject to App Tracking Transparency on iOS and a consent form in the EEA), purchase processing by the Apple App Store / Google Play, app usage statistics in Google Analytics for Firebase, and, for the ranking, the record and hero name of each finished life.

1. Information processed

There is no sign-up. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts or location.

There are no crash reports. The app downloads a notice file from ytlab.site and sends nothing when it does.

2. Retention

  • Game progress stays in on-device storage and is deleted with the app.
  • Ranking records are kept until you ask for their deletion. Google Analytics event data is deleted automatically after at most 14 months.

3. Advertising

  • Ads are a banner at the bottom of the home and hub screens, and, from the second life on, one full-screen ad when you leave the ending of a life. There are no ads on screens where you make a choice.
  • Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • On iOS, personalized or non-personalized ads are shown according to your App Tracking Transparency choice. In Europe a consent form is shown first and can be changed under Settings › Ad privacy settings.
  • Buying "Remove ads" turns all of these ads off.

4. Payments

  • In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store / Google Play. We never receive card or other financial details.
  • We sell extra classes (Mage, Rogue and Cleric, each or all three) and ad removal, all one-time, non-consumable purchases. No stats, items or random rewards are sold.
  • Purchases can be restored from Settings › Restore purchases.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your information and share it with no one beyond the processing above (AdMob ads, store payments, Google Analytics for Firebase statistics and Cloudflare for the ranking), except where required by law.

6. Your choices

  • Deleting the app deletes everything stored on the device.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • You can stop posting to the ranking under Settings › Post my lives to the ranking, and ask for deletion as described in section 7.

7. World ranking

When a life ends, its record goes to the weekly and all-time rankings. Turning off "Post my lives to the ranking" in Settings stops anything further from being sent.

  • Sent — the hero name (up to 10 characters, as typed), class, age at the end, best floor, guardians felled, titles earned, how the life ended, app language, and a random ranking ID issued by the server. The ranking ID is not a device or advertising identifier; there is no login or email.
  • Visible to others — the hero name with class, age, floor and score.
  • Reporting — long-press a row to report a name. Names reported by several players are hidden automatically, and names containing inappropriate words are filtered out by the server.
  • Abuse prevention — IP addresses are used for rate limiting and are not stored.
  • Deletion — send the "Ranking ID" shown at the bottom of Settings to contact@ytlab.site and we will delete every record posted under it, and the ID.
  • Location — processed and stored on Cloudflare (Workers, D1), and used for nothing else.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
