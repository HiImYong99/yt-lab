Pitwall Race Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026

한국어 · English

YT Lab (“the developer”) makes the mobile game Pitwall Race Manager (“the app”). This policy says what the app does with information. There is no account and no sign-in, and the app collects no personal information at all — no name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location.

1. What is not collected

The app sends nothing to the developer. There is no account, no developer-run server, and no identifier created for or received about a player. It asks for no permission to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location, and it does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS.

What Google handles in the course of showing an advertisement does not pass through the developer; what it is, is set out separately in 4. Advertising.

2. What stays on the device

Your progress — the team, the drivers, season results, in-game currency and settings — is written to a save file in the app's own storage on the device. It does not leave the device and is not synchronised to any cloud. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game events (a race finished, a screen opened) are written to a diagnostic file in the same storage. That file is never transmitted and the developer cannot read it.

3. Purchases

The app sells in-game items. Payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and the developer never receives payment details of any kind. The app uses only the transaction identifier the store hands back, on the device, so that a purchase already granted is not granted twice.

Purchase records and refunds are governed by each store's own policy.

4. Advertising

The app shows rewarded advertising only: a video plays when, and only when, a player chooses to watch one from a button on screen. There is no banner and no interstitial, and the game can be played from beginning to end without ever seeing an advertisement.

Advertising is served by Google AdMob. In requesting and showing one, Google may collect and use device information (model, operating system, language), the IP address and the approximate region inferred from it, an advertising identifier, and a record of advertisements shown, watched or dismissed. See how Google uses information in advertising.

On iOS the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. On Android the Google Play advertising ID (AAID) may be used; it can be reset, and personalised advertising turned off, in the device's own settings.

The developer receives none of this data. What the developer sees is an aggregate performance report — impressions, revenue — from which no individual player can be identified.

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form on first launch, and are served either personalised or non-personalised advertising according to their answer. That answer can be reopened and changed at any time from Settings → Advertising privacy in the app.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Since no personal information is collected at all, no processing requiring parental consent takes place.

6. Your rights

The developer holds no personal information about you, so there is nothing to request access to, correct or erase. The data on your device can be cleared from the app's settings or by deleting the app.

7. Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with its effective date. Material changes are also noted in the app's release notes.
