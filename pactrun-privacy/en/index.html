Privacy Policy

한국어 · English

YT Lab ("we") operates the mobile game PactRun (the "app"). This policy explains what information the app processes and how.

1. Information we process

The app has no account or login. Your progress (runs, codex, sanctuary, settings) is stored only on your device. The following is sent to our servers:

ItemWhenPurpose
Nickname (entered by you in Settings; a default is used otherwise)When a Daily Challenge endsShown on the daily leaderboard
Daily Challenge result (wave reached, elapsed time, lead creature, date)When a Daily Challenge endsRanking
Random device identifier (generated by the app)First Daily Challenge submissionOne record per device per day
Crash diagnostics (app version, device model, OS version, stack trace)When the app crashesFixing bugs
Advertising identifier and ad-serving dataWhen an ad is shownAd delivery and measurement (see section 3)
Regular runs send nothing to our servers. We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, precise location or contacts. The random device identifier is not the advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID) and is not used to track you across apps or services. The app downloads game-balance values via Firebase Remote Config; no personal data is sent in that request.

2. Storage and retention

  • Daily leaderboard records are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) for as long as that day's leaderboard is shown.
  • Crash diagnostics are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days.
  • On-device data (saved run, codex and sanctuary progress, settings) stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app, or immediately via "Delete save data" in Settings.
  • You may ask us to delete leaderboard records using the contact below.

3. Advertising

The app shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. The ads SDK may process the advertising identifier, IP address and device information for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement. Users in the EU/EEA and UK are asked for consent first through Google's User Messaging Platform. On iOS, App Tracking Transparency determines whether ads may be personalised. Purchasing the Remove Ads in-app product removes banners and grants rewards without watching ads. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store / Google Play; we do not store payment details.

4. Service providers

We do not sell personal data. We use the following processors:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — leaderboard storage, crash diagnostics, remote configuration. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — advertising. Google advertising policies

5. Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your leaderboard records by sending your nickname and the record date to the address below. Ad personalisation can be controlled in your device settings (iOS: Allow Tracking; Android: opt out of ads personalisation).

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over (14 and over in the Republic of Korea). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: contact@ytlab.site
