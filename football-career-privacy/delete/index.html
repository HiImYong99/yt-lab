설정 화면 맨 아래의 ID(없다면 구단주 닉네임과 대략의 가입 시기)를 적어 contact@ytlab.site로 보내 주세요. 확인 후 7일 안에 삭제합니다.

  • 구단주 정보(닉네임·꾸미기·레벨), 골드 잔액, 구단 시설, 아이템

남는 정보

구매한 골드·상품은 삭제와 함께 사라지며 환불되지 않습니다. 광고 제거 같은 스토어 구매는 같은 스토어 계정으로 '구매 복원'하면 다시 받을 수 있습니다.

Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open the app and open Settings.
  2. Tap Link account.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom, read the notice, then tap Permanently delete.

Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

If you can't open the app

Email contact@ytlab.site with the id shown at the foot of the Settings screen (or your owner nickname and roughly when you started). We delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your owner profile (nickname, cosmetics, level), gold balance, club facilities and items
  • The server's automatic backup (players, shelf, titles, game funds)
  • Submitted careers, ranking and daily challenge records, and your league club
  • Apple and Google account links (for Apple, the Sign in with Apple authorisation is revoked)
  • Usage analytics records and notification registration

What is kept

  • Match results that are part of other players' league and cup records, with your names removed.
  • Store transaction ids, linked to no one, so a purchase cannot be credited twice.

Gold and items you bought are lost with the account and are not refunded. Store purchases such as ad removal come back with "Restore purchases" on the same store account.
