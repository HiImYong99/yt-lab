Football Career Sim: Be a Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective 2 September 2026

한국어 · English

YT Lab ("the developer") makes the mobile game Football Career Sim: Be a Pro ("the app"). The app has no accounts and sends nothing to a server. This policy explains that, and the one exception, which is advertising.

1. What is processed

There is no sign-up. The app does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts or location. Only the following is processed.

DataProcessed byPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA / AAID)Google AdMobServing and measuring the single ad shown at the end of a career
Purchase receiptsApple App Store / Google PlayConfirming and restoring the remove-ads and style-pack purchases
Game progress (your shelf of retired players, settings, what you own)Your devicePicking up where you left off
Careers and the shelf are stored on your device only. The developer neither collects nor can see how you play. There is no analytics SDK and no crash reporting in this app.

2. Storage

  • Everything is kept in on-device storage (shared_preferences) and is removed when you delete the app.
  • The developer runs no server for this app.

3. Advertising

  • An ad is shown once, when a career ends. There are no banners and nothing between seasons.
  • Google AdMob may process the advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • On iOS, personalised or non-personalised ads follow your App Tracking Transparency choice. In Europe a consent form is shown first.
  • Buying "Remove ads" stops ads being shown.

4. Payments

  • In-app purchases are handled by the App Store and Google Play. The developer never receives card or other financial details.
  • Two products are sold - remove ads and the style pack - both one-off, non-consumable purchases.
  • Purchases can be recovered with "Restore purchases" in the app.

5. Sharing

The developer does not sell user data and shares nothing beyond the processing described above (AdMob, app-store billing), except where required by law.

6. Your choices

  • Deleting the app deletes everything stored on the device. There is nothing held elsewhere to request the deletion of.
  • The advertising identifier can be reset or limited in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking; Android: Settings > Ads).

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. About the names in the game

Every league, club, player and manager name in the app is invented and is not connected to any real organisation or person.
