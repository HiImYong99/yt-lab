Football Career Sim: Be a Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective 2 September 2026

한국어 · English

YT Lab ("the developer") makes the mobile game Football Career Sim: Be a Pro ("the app"). The app has no accounts and sends nothing to a server. This policy explains that, and the one exception, which is advertising.

1. What is processed

There is no sign-up. The app does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts or location. Only the following is processed.

DataProcessed byPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA / AAID)Google AdMobServing and measuring the bottom banner and the end-of-career ad
Purchase receiptsApple App Store / Google PlayConfirming and restoring the remove-ads and position-pack purchases
Game progress (your shelf of retired players, settings, what you own)Your devicePicking up where you left off
Player name, career record, anonymous idThis app's ranking server (Cloudflare)The global ranking - only if you opt in. See section 7
Careers and the shelf are stored on your device only. The developer neither collects nor can see how you play. There is no analytics SDK and no crash reporting in this app.

2. Storage

  • Everything is kept in on-device storage (shared_preferences) and is removed when you delete the app.
  • Nothing reaches a server of ours unless you enter the ranking. Section 7 covers how long that is kept and how to have it deleted.

3. Advertising

  • Ads are a bottom banner and one at the end of a career. Nothing between seasons, and nothing on top of a screen where you are choosing.
  • Google AdMob may process the advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • On iOS, personalised or non-personalised ads follow your App Tracking Transparency choice. In Europe a consent form is shown first.
  • Buying "Remove ads" stops ads being shown.

4. Payments

  • In-app purchases are handled by the App Store and Google Play. The developer never receives card or other financial details.
  • Two products are sold - remove ads and the position pack - both one-off, non-consumable purchases.
  • Purchases can be recovered with "Restore purchases" in the app.

5. Sharing

The developer does not sell user data and shares nothing beyond the processing described above (AdMob, app-store billing, and the Cloudflare processing that the ranking uses if you opt in), except where required by law.

6. Your choices

  • Deleting the app deletes everything stored on the device.
  • Anything you entered into the ranking can be deleted on request - see section 7.
  • The advertising identifier can be reset or limited in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking; Android: Settings > Ads).

7. Global ranking

The app offers an opt-in global ranking. Only from a device that has agreed to take part, the following is sent to our ranking server:

  • The player's name (up to 16 characters). The game generates one to suit the chosen nationality; you can keep it or type your own
  • Your career's play record (position, club, per-season appearances, goals, assists, and rating, national-team caps, retirement age, peak rating, earnings, and similar in-game stats)
  • An anonymous id (uid) issued by our service - not a device or advertising identifier, and it changes if you delete and reinstall the app

Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, and email addresses are not sent by this feature.

  • Retention - the information above is kept indefinitely, until a deletion request is made.
  • Deletion requests - send the id shown at the bottom of the ranking card on the home screen to the contact address below, and we will delete every record that id created on our server: its submitted careers, the reports it filed, the reports filed against its own careers, and any player it blocked. The one thing this does not delete: if another player blocked this id, that block is their own setting, not this id's data, and it stays in place.
  • Where this is processed - the information above is processed and stored on Cloudflare (Workers and D1), and is not shared with any other third party for this purpose.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. About the names in the game

Every league, club, player and manager name in the app is invented and is not connected to any real organisation or person.
