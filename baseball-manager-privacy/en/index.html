YT Lab ("the developer") operates Squeeze Play: Baseball Manager 2.0 (야구감독 키우기) ("the app"). There is no sign-up or login, and no email address, phone number or real name is required. Server features use random device and owner identifiers.

2. Storage and cloud save

  • Game data is stored on your device. The native app automatically uploads a save copy to the developer's server for restoration after reinstalling. The restore account is a random identifier stored in the device keychain or keystore.
  • Rankings, wallets, save copies and consented usage analytics are processed and stored on the server. You can request deletion as described below. Deleting the app deletes device data but does not itself request deletion of server data.
  • Settings > Move owner lets you transfer your owner using a code valid for seven days.

3. Advertising

  • The native app uses a Google AdMob bottom banner and a full-screen ad every sixth season after the first career. You can choose a rewarded ad for 10 extra rolls.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT) applies on iOS, and the UMP consent form applies in the European Economic Area (EEA). See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Remove ads removes the banner and full-screen ads. Optional rewarded ads are separate.

4. Payments

  • Apple and Google process payments, and Toss processes payments in the mini app. The developer does not receive card or other financial details. Our server verifies receipts to credit gold and tickets.
  • Products include Remove ads, the Manager type pack (Pitching Mind and Analyst), the Supporter pack (shelf capacity 10→50 and a badge), gold packs and roll tickets. Tickets are a paid random item used for starting-stat rerolls; odds are shown in the app.

5. Third-party processing

The developer does not sell user data. Cloudflare processes server data for the features above, and Apple, Google and Toss process payments or advertising. Information may be provided where required by law.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • To request deletion of server data, send the server ID shown in Settings to contact@ytlab.site. Deleting the app deletes device data.
  • You can opt out of rankings at the foot of the Rankings screen and turn off usage analytics in Settings. You can limit or reset your advertising identifier in device settings.

7. Rankings

Weekly, monthly and all-time rankings feature retired careers. The manager name, manager type, club, avatar, retirement age, stats, titles, and season wins and losses, places and targets are uploaded to the server. Names are generated; a name you type is sent only with your separate consent. Turning off ranking participation stops future record submissions.

A blocklist filters inappropriate names, and report and block features are available. A record is hidden after three reports.

8. Usage analytics

Only if you consent on first launch, usage events and a random install ID are sent to our own server to improve the game. They do not include names or advertising identifiers. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings. The Toss mini app does not use usage analytics.

9. Toss mini app

Toss handles advertising and payments in the Apps-in-Toss mini app "야구감독 키우기". The anonymous Toss login key is used only to link the wallet. Toss grants the points for the "Play for 3 minutes and get 2 Toss points" promotion.

10. Children and game names

The app is not directed at children. All league, club, player and manager names in the game are fictional.
