About the game

Football Career Sim is a mobile football (soccer) career simulation. A seventeen-year-old from the academy fights for a place off the bench, moves clubs, earns a national team call-up and decides when to retire.

When the career ends, the story continues. Retired players join the squad of your club in the Weekly League, where the players you raised face other clubs and fight for promotion.

Features

One player, one whole career

Play as a goalkeeper, defender, midfielder, winger or striker, month by month from the youth team to retirement.

Growth Cards

Every two seasons, pick one of three cards to raise your stats, lift a hidden ceiling or earn a reward by meeting a challenge.

Weekly League

Prepare on Monday, play the league from Tuesday to Friday, then the cup at the weekend. Each week decides promotion and relegation, up to World Class.

Friend matches and Daily Challenge

Challenge friends' clubs by code, or take the same player as everyone else and compete for the top score over eight seasons.

A life off the pitch

Spend your savings on cars, homes, family, hobbies and businesses as your career grows.

A fictional football world

All leagues, clubs, players and managers in the game are original creations.
