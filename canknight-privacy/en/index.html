Privacy Policy

Effective: July 28, 2026

한국어 · English

YT Lab ("we", "the developer") operates the mobile game CanKnight ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app processes and how.

1. Information we process

The app has no sign-up or login. It processes only the following:

ItemWhenPurpose
Knight name (nickname)Auto-generated on first play (e.g. Knight1234); editable by youDisplay on the weekly leaderboard
Game records (score, distance, week)At the end of each runWeekly leaderboard ranking
Random device identifier (generated by the app)On first leaderboard entryOne entry per device (dedupe)
Country/region code (inferred from device locale)On leaderboard entryCountry code display
Runs are automatically posted to the weekly global leaderboard under your auto-generated knight name. We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, precise location, or contacts. The random device identifier is not an advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID) and is never used to track you across apps or services. Country/region is a code (e.g. KR) inferred from your device's locale settings — no GPS or precise location. Nothing is transmitted while the device is offline.

2. Advertising

The app shows non-personalized ads only, served by Google AdMob.

  • We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID) and do not request iOS App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • To serve ads, Google may process coarse location (IP-based) and device information for frequency capping and fraud prevention. See Google's advertising policies.
  • Users in the EEA/UK are asked for consent via Google's consent management platform (UMP).

3. Storage and retention

  • Leaderboard entries are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore).
  • On-device data (best score, discovered secrets, settings, the random identifier) stays on your device.
  • Retention: as long as the leaderboard purpose remains. Server entries may persist after you delete the app; you can request deletion via the contact below.

4. Third-party processors

We never sell personal information. We use the following processors:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — leaderboard storage and retrieval. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — non-personalized ad serving. AdMob privacy

5. Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your leaderboard entries. Email us with your knight name and we will handle it.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version with a new effective date on this page.

8. Contact

Privacy inquiries and deletion requests: contact@ytlab.site
