Privacy Policy

한국어 · English

Effective 4 November 2026 · Sack Race Football Manager 2.2.1

YT Lab provides a football manager career game without a login requirement. We do not require your real name, contacts or precise location. Manager and owner names you enter, game records, a random service ID and data needed for the features below are processed.

Game saves and online features

Careers, the archive and settings are stored on your device. Automatic backup may also store save data on Cloudflare servers under a random service ID. Reinstallation can restore data when the same ID remains available. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete server backups or online records.

Rankings, public owner profiles and the daily challenge display names, characters, manager career records, scores and ranks to other players. Service IDs, progression, gift messages, purchase validation information, reports and blocks are also processed to provide gifts, rewards, purchase rights and abuse prevention. Avoid entering real names or contact details. Use the participation settings and report/block tools provided in the app. Ranking participation settings operate separately from backup and purchase-rights processing.

Usage analytics and crash reporting

According to your in-app usage-statistics choice, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics process screen and gameplay events, app-instance identifiers, device/OS/app versions, language, approximate country or region, and error/crash diagnostics. These support improvements to game flow and stability. You can change your usage-statistics choice in Settings.

Notifications

Notification features use Apple/Android notification services and Firebase Cloud Messaging installation IDs and registration tokens. When server notifications are enabled, notification preferences, language, time zone, recent activity and necessary career-progress information may be processed on the server. Notifications can be disabled in the app and OS settings. Turning them off requests deletion of the server notification token held by the app.

Advertising and purchases

Google AdMob and mediation networks used by the native app may process advertising identifiers, ad interactions and device information to serve and measure ads. Mediation may include Unity and Mintegral. Your iOS ATT choice and applicable regional ad-consent choices apply. Apps-in-Toss uses Toss-provided advertising and billing mechanisms and their related policies.

Apple App Store and Google Play process purchases for ad removal, the manager type pack, supporter products and consumable gold/reroll products. Our server may validate receipts and order/transaction identifiers and restore or manage purchase rights. The developer does not receive card numbers.

Retention and deletion requests

Regular app storage is removed when you delete it or uninstall the app. A service ID stored in iOS Keychain may remain after reinstallation. Server backups, records and purchase-rights information may be retained for providing and restoring features or until a deletion request is processed. Firebase, advertising and payment services apply their own retention and deletion policies. Data is transmitted using encrypted connections such as HTTPS.

For server-record deletion or privacy requests, contact contact@ytlab.site with the service/ranking ID shown in Settings. Support inquiries include the message and contact information needed to reply. We do not sell user data or knowingly collect children's personal information. Leagues, clubs and people in the game are fictional.

Related policies: Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
