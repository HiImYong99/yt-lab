YT Lab ("we", "the developer") operates the mobile game Ban Pick Manager ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app processes and how. The app has no account or sign-in, and does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

All game data — career saves and settings — is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. Deleting the app removes it.

2. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads (rewarded and interstitial) via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the advertising identifier, coarse location, and device/usage information for ad delivery, measurement, and personalization. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.

  • iOS: your device advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalized ads only if you allow tracking via App Tracking Transparency. Every feature of the app works even if you decline.
  • You can reset the advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings at any time.
  • Purchasing the Remove Ads product disables interstitial and banner ads.

3. In-app purchases

The app offers in-app purchases such as in-game funds, convenience packs, and Remove Ads. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store; we never collect or store your payment details. Purchases are restored through your App Store account.

4. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — ad delivery and measurement
  • Apple App Store — in-app purchase processing

5. Data retention and deletion

All game data lives only on your device and is removed when you delete the app. We do not keep your game data on any server.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
