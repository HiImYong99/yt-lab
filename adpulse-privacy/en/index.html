AdMeter

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

한국어 · English

YT Lab ("the developer") operates the mobile app AdMeter ("the app"). The app is a dashboard that lets you sign in with your own Google account to view your own AdMob and AdSense ad revenue. The developer runs no servers of its own; your revenue data and credentials never leave your device.

1. Information we process

ItemWhenPurpose · storage
Basic Google profile (name, email, photo)On Google sign-inShown in the app. Stored on your device only
AdMob report data (estimated earnings, read-only)Only after you connect AdMobDashboard display. Cached on your device only
AdSense report data (estimated earnings, read-only)Only after you connect AdSenseDashboard display. Cached on your device only
Advertising identifier, coarse location, app interaction, diagnosticsWhen ads are shown (section 3)Ad serving and measurement, diagnostics
Information received from Google APIs is used for a single purpose: rendering your dashboard on your device. We do not sell, share, or transfer your revenue data to third parties, do not use it for advertising, and no human reads it.

2. Google API access (Limited Use disclosure)

After sign-in, the app requests the following read-only scopes only with your explicit consent:

  • admob.readonly — read your AdMob reports
  • adsense.readonly — read your AdSense reports

AdMeter's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the advertising identifier, coarse location, and device/usage information for ad serving, measurement, and personalization. See the Google Privacy Policy and how AdMob uses data.

  • iOS: the device advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalized ads only if you allow tracking via App Tracking Transparency.
  • EU/EEA/UK: consent is collected via the Google UMP consent form; personalized or non-personalized ads are shown accordingly.
  • You can reset the advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings at any time.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Cached revenue data and sign-in state live only on your device; signing out or deleting the app removes them.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at Google Account > Security > Third-party access.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In (Google Identity) — authentication
  • Google AdMob API · AdSense Management API — read-only revenue reports
  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising

6. Children

The app is intended for ad publishers and developers and is not directed at children under 13.
